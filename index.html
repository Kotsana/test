Fighting e-Waste, Forging a Sustainable Future

Countering e-Waste in a digital world

  • THE MISSIONAs IWB, we aim to put an end to e-waste and creating a sustainable future for the youth. Our goal is to reduce e-waste by proving a platform for technology business to reclaim the discarted electronics and re using them to create even better products.

  • THE PROBLEM.E-waste as the name suggests, is the discard of electronic equipment, from office equipment being printers to phones and radios by commoners. In this technology driven world or a digital world, e-waste is slowly growing as more people are exposed to new technologies daily, thereby leaving behind the old. Of course there is no problem in adapting or moving to newer technologies, the problem is we often do not know where to leave or discard the electronic equipment of old technologies that we leave behind.
